# Medoro Pharmacy – YouTube upload sheet

Upload these 26 videos to your channel, then send Claude the 26 links (or one playlist link). The website switches to YouTube playback as soon as the IDs are added; until then it keeps playing its own copies.

**Settings for every video**

- Visibility: **Public** or **Unlisted** (both work on the website; Public also brings YouTube search traffic). Not Private: private videos can't be embedded.
- Audience: *No, it's not made for kids*.
- Allow embedding: **on** (Show more → License → Allow embedding). Required for the website.
- Language: Hindi. Category: Science & Technology (or Education).
- Subtitles are already burned into the picture. Uploading the matching `.srt` from `subtitles_optional/` is optional (it helps YouTube search); leave captions off by default.
- Thumbnail: the custom thumbnails need a verified channel (phone verification). Matching files are in `thumbnails/` (same names as the videos).
- Add all 26 to one playlist: **Medoro Pharmacy – Complete Product Tour (Hindi)**, in part order.

Fill in the YouTube link column as you go and send the table back.
